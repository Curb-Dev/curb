DELIBERATELY NOT VENDORED
  Uniswap/v4-hooks-public. PONS's MemeHook imports BaseHook from it, and
  BaseHook is NOT in v4-periphery 1.0.4 - upstream moved it out. So a v4 hook
  of our own would need this package.

  It was installed, measured at 32,065 files, and then REMOVED because the
  hook decision is open - see DIFF_TABLE.txt C1 and ROADMAP.txt Phase 0 E.

  [CORRECTED] An earlier version of this note, and the commit message that
  removed it, treated 32,065 files as the cost of writing a v4 hook. That is
  wrong. BaseHook.sol imports only from v4-core and v4-periphery:
    Hooks, IPoolManager, IHooks, BalanceDelta, PoolKey, BeforeSwapDelta,
    ModifyLiquidityParams/SwapParams   (all @uniswap/v4-core)
    ImmutableState                     (@uniswap/v4-periphery)
  Nothing from briefcase. The 32k figure is the SIZE OF THAT REPOSITORY,
  which vendors briefcase for its own test suite - not the dependency a hook
  actually pulls in. The needed files can be taken from a pinned source with
  their licences.

  DO NOT let that number weigh on the venue decision. It measured the wrong
  thing.
  [ ] Reinstall when the venue decision lands, and record its revision then.

OPEN TEST WORK - NOT DONE, DO NOT MARK COMPLETE
  [x] Multi-trade accounting invariants - DONE, test/CurveInvariants.t.sol.
      Six invariants over randomised buy/sell/sweep/donate sequences, each
      256 runs x 500 calls. afterInvariant() refuses a run in which no buy,
      sell or sweep actually succeeded, so an all-reverting campaign cannot
      report green. Costs ~39s, the bulk of the suite's runtime.
  [ ] The sweep-preserves-price result is proven for buybackEnabled = false,
      our MVP setting. With buyback on, the sweep trades against the curve's
      own reserve and moves both sides; that case is untested.
