================================================================================
Written   8 October 2026
Status    For use from the moment DEPLOY.txt STEP 4 lands. Nothing is deployed
          yet. The names and phone numbers marked ........ are filled in
          before the day IN A PRIVATE COPY of this file, kept outside the
          repository and never committed - not here (WHO says why).

WHAT THIS IS
  The playbook for an incident after the deployment: a contract bug or an
  exploit, a bad launch, an issuer stopping an asset, a wrong holder-sharing
  payout, a stolen key, or the site or the domain taken over. What to look at
  first, which levers exist and what each one CANNOT do, who signs, and what
  users are told.
  A deployment that fails half way is DEPLOY.txt, IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG.
  Holder sharing's own alerts are services/sharer/RUNBOOK.md parts 13 and
  14, and this file points there rather than repeating them. How a Safe
  signature is made safely is SAFE_OPS.txt.

THE FIRST THING TO KNOW: NOTHING CAN BE PAUSED, AND THAT IS BY DESIGN
  No CURB contract has a pause, an upgrade path or an admin who can move
  somebody's funds. The token has no owner; the curve has no owner; the
  graduated pool is an ordinary Uniswap v4 pool; the lock has no withdrawal.
  Launching is open to anyone on every factory, and factories can never be
  removed from the registry. So in an incident there is no switch that stops
  trading, stops launching, or gives anything back - on any launch, ever.
  A bug in a contract every launch shares reaches launches that already
  exist, and the fix is a NEW contract that only new launches use.
  What does exist is listed below, with its limit next to it. Read the limit
  before using the lever: most of them stop the SITE from leading people
  somewhere, not the chain from doing something.

================================================================================
TRIAGE - THE FIRST THIRTY MINUTES
================================================================================
  1. WHAT KIND IS IT? Pick one; the playbooks below follow this list.
       a contract bug or an exploit         a key stolen or lost
       a bad or misleading launch           the site or the domain taken over
       an issuer stopping an asset          a launch stuck in Waiting
       a wrong holder-sharing payout
  2. IS MONEY MOVING RIGHT NOW? Read the chain, not the page: Basescan for the
     contracts in ADDRESSES.txt, the transactions in the last blocks, the
     events. A page may be the thing that is wrong.
  3. REPRODUCE OFF CHAIN, NEVER ON IT. A fork of Base at the current block, on
     a port of its own - never the suite's pinned node, never a broadcast:
       base-anvil --fork-url <a Base RPC> --port 8560
     then the transaction, the call or the script against
     http://127.0.0.1:8560.
  4. WHAT IS AFFECTED? Which factories (paired assets), which launches
     (registry.launchSlice, the lens's launchPage), curve or pool, sharing or
     not. Write it down: the X post needs it.
  5. PICK THE LEVERS from the table below, and the playbook's order.
  6. START A LOG - times, transactions, decisions - from the first minute. It
     becomes the write-up.


================================================================================
THE LEVERS, AND THE LIMIT OF EACH
================================================================================
  WHO: "Safe A" is the policy Safe (it owns the hook, the catalog, the
  distributor factory and, after DEPLOY.txt STEP 4B, the registry); "a
  vetoer" is Safe B, the guardian key or Safe A; "anyone" is any address.

  THE SITE - web/config.js, one commit to main, live in minutes
    banner                 a notice at the top of every page
      who / how fast       whoever can publish the site / minutes
      cannot               reach anyone who does not use the site
    hiddenLaunches         takes a launch out of Launches, the hero and the
                           search
      cannot               stop its trading, or a direct link or explorer
                           from reaching it
    hiddenFactories        takes a paired asset out of the create form and the
                           lists - the halt PRE_MAINNET D4 describes
      cannot               stop anyone launching on that factory directly, or
                           trading what exists
    featured               the only launches a ?featured= link may put in the
                           front page's card; a link naming any other launch
                           is ignored. Take a launch out to stop such links
                           featuring it.
      cannot               choose what the plain front page features: the
                           registry's first ETH pair, unless hidden
    the router             change one digit of web/app.js's CURB_ROUTER
                           codehash and publish: the page sends nothing to a
                           router whose code does not hash to it, and takes
                           the longer flows (swap then buy; approve, sell,
                           unwrap)
      cannot               stop the router itself, which has no owner; anyone
                           can still call it
    take the site down     the host's own switch, or the domain pointed away
      cannot               stop the chain - and it also stops the claims,
                           the checks and the notice users need. Last resort,
                           and for a site that is no longer ours.

  HOLDER SHARING - the HolderDistributorFactory and the SharingCatalog
    veto / vetoMany        stops a pending root inside its hour
      who / how fast       a vetoer / at once
      cannot               touch an active root, which keeps paying
    freeze()               stops publishing and holds every pending root (each
                           can then be vetoed whatever its hour)
      who / how fast       a vetoer or Safe A / at once
      cannot               stop an active root's payouts; unfreezing takes
                           announceUnfreeze and 48 hours
    revokePublisher()      the publisher key can propose nothing more
      who / how fast       Safe A / at once
      cannot               bring a new publisher in under 48 hours
    catalog retire(f)      no new launch can switch to that distributor
                           version
      who / how fast       Safe A / at once
      cannot               change a launch already switched to it
    cancelPending()        withdraws an announced publisher, keeper payee or
                           unfreeze
      who / how fast       Safe A / at once, inside the 48 hours
  THE PLATFORM POLICY - the FeeHook
    announcePlatformRecipient / announcePlatformFeeBps, then execute after 24
    hours, within 7 days; cancel... withdraws one
      who / how fast       Safe A / 24 hours
      cannot               reach any launch made before the change: each keeps
                           the share and recipient it was created with
  THE REGISTRY
    addLauncher            admits a new paired asset (DeployAssetStack.s.sol)
      who / how fast       Safe A after STEP 4B (the deployer before) / at once
      cannot               remove one. There is no removal; 32 at most.
  ANYONE, WITHOUT ASKING
    Graduator.graduate(token)          completes a launch stuck in Waiting
                                       (the page's Complete the move)
    Graduator.noteReady(token)         notes a full, ungraduated launch
                                       stuck; the keeper sends it itself when
                                       graduate() would revert
    Graduator.rescueCurveFees(token)   pays a full curve's fees straight out
                                       when the issuer blocks the escrow, so
                                       it can graduate (DEPLOY.txt, A LAUNCH
                                       STRANDED BY THE ISSUER). Its
                                       creator-fee recipient and the platform
                                       recipient at once; anyone else an hour
                                       after noteReady(token).
    a distributor's harvest()          pulls a launch's income in; push and
                                       claim pay the active root's leaves from
                                       its published file. All without the
                                       keeper - but no new root without it.

  WHAT NOTHING CAN DO
    stop trading on any curve or pool       change any launch's terms
    stop launching on any factory           take liquidity out of a lock
    move or freeze anybody's tokens         recover a lost creator key
    undo a payout that was made             pay out more than a distributor
                                            received

PLAYBOOKS
================================================================================
  A CONTRACT BUG OR AN EXPLOIT
    1. Triage 1-4. Reproduce it on the fork; know which contracts and which
       launches before saying anything specific.
    2. Banner at once: what is affected, what to avoid ("do not buy on X
       pair", "do not approve Y"), that the contracts cannot be paused.
    3. Hide what leads people into it: hiddenLaunches, hiddenFactories, or
       the router out of CURB_ROUTER, whichever the bug lives in.
    4. Post on X (COMMS below), then update at fixed times.
    5. If holder sharing is involved, freeze it (Safe A or a vetoer).
    6. The fix is a new contract, reviewed and tested like the first: a new
       factory for a paired asset (DeployAssetStack.s.sol, admitted by Safe
       A), a new distributor version (catalog propose, 7 days). Existing
       launches stay on the old code; say so plainly.
  A BAD OR MISLEADING LAUNCH (a copy of a real brand, a launch posing as
  CURB's own, a launch whose links lead to a scam)
    hiddenLaunches, then a note on X if people are being misled. It keeps
    trading; the site stops pointing at it. $CURB is the token ADDRESSES.txt
    names (registry.launchAt(0), unless DEPLOY.txt's $CURB IS NOT LAUNCH ZERO
    happened) - say so when a copy circulates.
  AN ISSUER STOPPING AN ASSET (a pause, a blocklist)
    Nothing of ours routes around it (PRE_MAINNET D7). hiddenFactories for
    that asset, a banner naming it, and nothing else to sign. A full curve
    whose escrow is blocked: rescueCurveFees, then graduate. The platform
    Safe (the platform recipient) may rescue at once and is the quickest way
    out; anyone else an hour after Graduator.noteReady(token), which the
    keeper sends itself when graduate() would revert.
  A WRONG HOLDER-SHARING PAYOUT
    services/sharer/RUNBOOK.md part 13 (the alert table) and part 14. While a
    root is pending: veto. Once it became payable: freeze, then RUNBOOK part
    13's row for a root no longer vetoable. Know what the freeze costs:
    active roots keep paying, publishing stops for EVERY launch until the
    unfreeze (announced, then 48 hours), and nothing paid comes back.
  A KEY STOLEN OR LOST
    the publisher            RUNBOOK part 14: freeze and revokePublisher at
                             once; then announce the new publisher and the
                             unfreeze (48 hours each), and execute unfreeze()
                             before executePublisher(), which a freeze
                             refuses
    a pusher or the guardian RUNBOOK part 14 and part 12
    a Safe signer            SAFE_OPS.txt, REPLACING A SIGNER: swap it out
                             before the remaining keys fall below the
                             threshold
    the deployer             before STEP 4B it owns the registry: the Safe
                             accepts the handover at once (DEPLOY.txt STEP
                             4B). After it, the key owns only the buyback
                             vaults, which hold nothing and do nothing.
    the platform recipient   every launch made while it is the recipient pays
                             it forever. Safe A announces a new recipient now
                             (24 hours) so later launches pay the new one, and
                             whoever holds the old key claims what is owed
                             to it as soon as possible (SAFE_OPS.txt,
                             PLATFORM RECIPIENT).
    a creator's own key      nothing of ours can help: the creator wallet is
                             permanent. Say so if asked.
  THE SITE OR THE DOMAIN TAKEN OVER
    SAFE_OPS.txt, IF THE SITE IS NOT OURS ANY MORE. In short: take it down,
    tell people on X not to sign anything there, get the account back,
    republish from a known commit, check web/config.js and CURB_ROUTER
    against ADDRESSES.txt before the domain comes back.
  A LAUNCH STUCK IN WAITING (AutoGraduationFailed)
    The keeper finds it within ten minutes and sends Graduator.graduate()
    itself (DEPLOY.txt, IMMEDIATELY AFTER 3); it pages when it cannot, or
    when the call would revert. Anyone can also press Complete the move on
    its page. If it fails again, reproduce it on the fork to find why - an
    escrow blocked by the issuer needs Graduator.rescueCurveFees(token)
    first: the platform recipient may send it at once, anyone else an hour
    after Graduator.noteReady(token) - the keeper sends that note itself and
    its page says when the rescue opens.

COMMS
================================================================================
  WHERE, IN THIS ORDER
    1. X, from the project's account - the first place people look.
    2. The site's banner (web/config.js banner), pointing to the X thread.
    3. docs.md, if a fact on it changed.
  WHAT EACH POST SAYS - only what is known
    what happened, in one sentence; which contracts and which launches, by
    address; what users should do now (stop trading X, revoke the approval to
    Y with their wallet or a token-approval tool, nothing to do); what CURB
    can do and cannot do - the contracts cannot be paused, nothing can be
    reversed; when the next update comes.
  WHAT IT NEVER SAYS OR DOES
    asks anyone for a seed phrase, a private key or a transaction to sign;
    links to a "fix", a "migration" or a claim page; guesses at a cause or a
    figure not yet measured; promises an outcome. Anyone who sends people a
    link to sign "for the incident" is not us - say that in the first post.
  AFTERWARDS
    A write-up from the log: timeline, cause, what was done, what changes.
    Kept in the repository next to this file.
