1. WHERE NVDAc IS ASSUMED
================================================================================

1.1 THE CONTRACTS - LESS THAN EXPECTED, AND THE CONSTRAINT IS NOT WHERE IT LOOKS

    THERE IS NO NVDAc ADDRESS ANYWHERE IN src/. Every occurrence of the name in
    the contracts is a comment. The quote asset is a FIELD ON THE LAUNCH
    RECORD, and every consumer reads it from there.

    ALREADY ASSET-AGNOSTIC - these need no change to serve a second asset:

      LaunchRegistry   stores `address quoteToken` per launch [:83]. No
                       allowlist and no per-asset branch: register() checks
                       only that it is non-zero [:280] and not the launch token
                       itself [:285].
      Graduator        reads rec.quoteToken for the reserve transfer, the
                       currency ordering and the refund [:213, :218, :225,
                       :232-233, :287].
      FeeHook          stores quoteToken per POOL [:117] and derives which side
                       of the pair it is [:197, :200]. One hook already serves
                       any number of quote assets.
      LaunchLens       reads it from the record and rebuilds the pool key
                       [:97, :104-107].
      PositionLock,
      FeePolicy        no quote-asset term at all.

    BOUND TO ONE ASSET:

      CurbLaunchFactory  `address public immutable quoteToken` [:158, set :249,
                         used :312, :337]. One factory serves one asset.
      FeeEscrow          `address public immutable quoteToken` [:61, set :95];
                         creditToken and the claim path refuse every other
                         token with UnsupportedToken [:110, :194].

    BOUND TO ONE SET OF NUMBERS - the same mechanism, and the bigger half of
    the problem. All of these are immutable on the factory [:164-172]:

      supply, phantomQuote, graduationThreshold, poolFee,
      tickSpacing, hooks, protocolFeeRecipient, maxInternalPriceImpactBps

    So a factory is not merely "the NVDAc factory": it is one asset AND one
    parameter set. For multi-asset that is a feature, not an obstacle - see
    section 3 - because the parameters have to differ per asset anyway.

    THE REAL CONSTRAINT IS THE REGISTRY, AND IT IS EASY TO MISS.
    LaunchRegistry.wire() [:241] names ONE factory and ONE hook, is callable
    exactly once by the deployer, and VERIFIES the whole loop closes before
    accepting them - the launcher's graduator points back at this registry, the
    hook has code and the right permissions, the hook's poolOpener is that
    graduator, and hook and graduator agree on the PoolManager
    [the checks are documented at LaunchRegistry.sol:237-255, the block above
    wire() itself].

    Consequence: "just deploy a second factory" DOES NOT WORK against the
    registry that exists. A second factory cannot register into it. Any
    multi-asset design has to answer this, and the two honest answers are a
    second registry per asset or a registry that admits more than one factory.

1.2 DEPLOYMENT

    script/DeployCurb.s.sol:138   new FeeEscrow(NVDAC)

    That is the ONLY place the NVDAc address is written down in anything that

  There is no way to enumerate B20 on chain. base-std's IB20Factory declares
  exactly four functions - createB20, getB20Address, isB20, isB20Initialized -
  and none of them counts, indexes or lists. The precompile sits at
  0xB20f000000000000000000000000000000000000. A B20 address is 0xb2, ten zero
  bytes, then nine bytes that cannot be walked.

  So candidates came from outside and the CHAIN decided. Three public token
  lists (CoinGecko's Base list, 1inch's chain-8453 list, Uniswap's default Base

  recorded below - isB20(), symbol(), name(), decimals(), totalSupply(), the
  three transfer policy scopes, the policy registry, and every pool at three

  mismatch can be reported; it is never the evidence.

2.7 THE RISK THAT GOT WORSE, NOT BETTER

    it. Spreading across REGIMES - an equity, a wrapped asset with its own
    admin, a policy-free token - is the only thing measured here that would.


================================================================================
3. PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

3.1 WHAT THE SHAPE OF THE PROBLEM ACTUALLY IS

    Two things must be per-asset and one thing must not.

      PER ASSET, necessarily:  the quote token, and the parameter set
                               (threshold, phantom, pool tier, tick spacing),
                               because section 2.3 shows they cannot be shared.
      PER ASSET, by current
      construction:            the escrow, which refuses any other token.
      NOT PER ASSET:           discovery. One registry is what makes browse a
                               single lens call; fragmenting it is a product
                               regression, not an implementation detail.

3.2 THREE OPTIONS

    OPTION A - A WHOLE STACK PER ASSET
      A registry, factory, graduator, hook and escrow per asset.
      FOR   no contract changes at all; complete isolation - a halted or
            restricted asset cannot affect another.
      AGAINST
            a hook per registry means MINING A HOOK SALT PER ASSET [:131];
            discovery fragments into N registries and the browse page becomes
            an aggregator over N lenses; N escrows to sweep and N wirings to
            verify; the platform recipient is repeated N times, which multiplies
            the surface A6 is deciding.
      VERDICT  the honest fallback if 3.3 is judged too invasive, but it pays
            in the place the product is weakest - discovery - to avoid touching
            one function.

    OPTION B - ONE REGISTRY, A FACTORY PER ASSET   << RECOMMENDED >>
      One registry, one graduator, one hook. A factory per asset, each keeping
      its immutable parameters and its own escrow. The registry admits more
      than one launcher.
      CHANGE REQUIRED
            LaunchRegistry.wire() becomes "add a launcher": the SAME loop
            verification runs per factory, the hook stays single and is fixed
            at the first call, and each factory is added once and never
            changed. This is the most safety-critical function in the file and
            the change must keep every check it performs today.
      FOR   discovery stays ONE registry and ONE lens call, so browse, the
            launch page and the whole browser suite keep working unchanged;
            one hook, so no salt mining per asset; parameters stay IMMUTABLE
            per asset, which is what D1 bought.
      AGAINST
            it touches a write-once function that currently cannot be called
            twice, and "callable more than once" is exactly the property that
            made it safe. The mitigation is that each launcher is still
            add-only and never replaceable, and the loop check is unchanged -
            but this needs its own tests at the registry, including a mutation
            run, before it is believed.

    OPTION C - ONE FACTORY, PARAMETERS IN A TABLE
      A mapping from asset to parameter set inside one factory.
      AGAINST  it converts immutable parameters into mutable state with an
            administrator. This project has deliberately spent effort removing
            exactly that - C3 records that nobody can point a creator's
            income at an address of their choosing, with no admin recovery.
            NOT RECOMMENDED.

3.3 THE ESCROW

    Keep ONE ESCROW PER ASSET, unchanged. FeeEscrow holds money and refuses
    every token but its own; making it multi-token means editing the credit and
    claim paths of a contract that custodies fees, for no gain the per-asset
    instance does not already give. A2's closure then carries per asset, and a
    restriction on one asset cannot strand another's fees.

    The platform recipient (A6) stays SHARED across assets - it is one decision,
    not N.

3.4 WHAT THE INTERFACE HAS TO GROW

    - the create form gains a quote-asset choice, from a SHORT EXPLICIT LIST -
      not "any address", because section 2.3 shows an asset without a route is
      a launch nobody can enter.
    - the choice is fixed at launch and read back from the record everywhere
      else; the curve and the graduated pool use the same asset by construction,
      since both already read rec.quoteToken.
    - labels come from symbol() instead of the 106 literal occurrences above.
    - the ETH route becomes per-asset: a one-hop WETH path where one exists
      (NVDAc today) and a two-hop WETH->USDC->asset path otherwise. THIS IS THE
      ONLY GENUINELY NEW ENGINEERING in the interface and it is unmeasured.
    - browse gains a filter by quote asset once more than one exists.

3.5 WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE

    The fee model, in full. The registry record's shape. The hook. The
    graduator. The lock. The lens's ABI. The browser suite's selectors.


================================================================================
4. IMPLEMENTATION ORDER, AND WHAT EACH ASSET MUST PASS
================================================================================

4.1 ORDER - each step is useful on its own and none needs the next

    1  DECIDE THE ASSET LIST. Product, not engineering, and it comes first
       because everything downstream is sized by it. Section 2 is the input;
       this file does not make the choice.

    2  MEASURE EACH CHOSEN ASSET THE WAY D3 MEASURED NVDAc: depth, price
       impact and round-trip cost at the trade sizes the product intends. Only
       then can the threshold, the phantom and the tier be chosen - per asset,
       from its own table. Copying 60 is refused by 2.3(c).

    3  REGISTRY: admit more than one launcher, keeping every check wire()
       performs today, add-only and never replaceable. Tests at the registry
       in the existing fork suite, plus a mutation run on the new gate. No new
       harness.

    4  DEPLOY SCRIPT: parameterise the stack per asset - quote token, escrow,
       factory parameters - with the registry, graduator and hook deployed once.

    5  INTERFACE: asset choice at create, labels from symbol(), per-asset route.
       The two-hop route is built and MEASURED against the deployed router on
       the fork before it is offered, the way the one-hop route was.

    6  PER-ASSET LIFECYCLE, below. An asset is not supported until it passes.

    A6, A4, A3 and A1 are unaffected in kind by any of this; A3's scope grows
    with the asset count, which is a reason to fix the list before the audit
    rather than after.

4.2 THE LIFECYCLE EACH ASSET MUST PASS BEFORE IT IS OFFERED

    Nothing here is a new kind of test - it is the existing fork and browser
    coverage, run against that asset.

    ON THE CURVE
      1  a launch is created with that asset as quote, and the record carries it
      2  a buy delivers exactly what was quoted, and the tax splits 50 bps to
         the platform and T-50 to the creator, in that asset
      3  a sell does the same on the gross output side
      4  the books close: reserve == owed + tradeable, independently accounted

    GRADUATION
      5  the threshold is crossed and graduation runs
      6  the pool is opened on the right pair, with the right hook, at the
         chosen tier and tick spacing
      7  the LP position is locked for the full duration
      8  the remainder is burned and the leftover quote is refunded

    IN THE POOL
      9  a buy is charged on the asset side in beforeSwap
      10 a sell is charged on the gross asset output in afterSwap
      11 the recipients are checked PER LEG, not by the sum

    THE MONEY OUT
      12 the sweep credits the escrow for that asset
      13 the platform claims; the creator claims; neither can take the other's

    THE WAY IN
      14 the ETH route end to end through the deployed UniversalRouter, with
         the fill MEASURED from the swap deltas rather than inferred from a
         minimum-output bound
      15 a routed revert unwinds whole, leaving the trader what they started
         with

    THE ISSUER
      16 the policy refusal path for that asset, provoked through the policy
         admin the way C1 did for NVDAc, and surfaced in the interface

    FAILING ANY OF 1-16 MEANS THE ASSET IS NOT SUPPORTED. "It is a B20 token
    like NVDAc" is not evidence - 2.3 is the counter-example, where three of
    four share every property that can be read off the asset and none of them
    share the route.

5. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DECIDE

    - which assets are adopted, and how many
    - any threshold, phantom, r, pool tier or tick spacing for any asset
    - the conversion route for any asset but NVDAc, which exists already
    - whether a dollar entry is offered alongside the ETH one
    - anything about the fee model, which is frozen

    All of these are product decisions. The measurements in section 2 are the
