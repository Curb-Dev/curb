Where the platform's fees go

80%buys $CURB and burns it
20%runs the platform

A treasury policy, decided on 22 September 2026 — not code. The treasury carries it out by hand, once enough fee income has built up: on a threshold, not on a date.

  • No contract splits the platform's income 80/20, buys $CURB or burns it. The contracts collect the platform's income — its share of each trade; launching itself is free — and pay it to the platform's address. They do nothing further with it. Every launch's buyback share is set to zero, so no launch pays into a buyback.
  • Some of the platform's fees wait in the contracts until they are claimed; the amount waiting is shown below.
  • No $CURB buyback or burn has happened yet.

The fee rules in short

Creator tax
1–5%, picked by the creator at launch and fixed for that launch. Charged in the paired asset on every buy and every sell, on the curve and in the pool.
Platform share
Part of the tax, never added on top. At most 0.80%; it started at 0.50%. The creator keeps the rest of the tax.
Changing the share
Announced on chain 24 hours ahead, and lapses unless applied within 7 days after that. It reaches only launches created after it applies.
Each launch
Keeps the share and the recipient in force when it was created, for as long as it exists.
Pool LP fee
0.05%, only after graduation, and not part of the tax. It goes to the pool's liquidity providers, mostly the launch's own position, whose fees the creator can collect at any time. That position is locked forever: nobody can withdraw it.
Launch fee
None. Launching costs gas only.
Buyback in the contracts
0%. The 80/20 above is the treasury's policy, not the contracts'.
