Who earns your tax

You keep the creator share. You can still give it to holders later, from the token page; that switch is one-way.

  • Your creator share becomes 0%, permanently.
  • Holders receive your tax share, before and after graduation, plus the locked position's LP fees.
  • The LP principal stays locked forever; nobody can withdraw it.
  • Shares are time-weighted and paid about hourly. At most 2% of each payout covers gas. Every payout is public and can be stopped by CURB for 1 hour.
  • Payouts need CURB's keeper to keep publishing them; unpaid shares wait in the distributor.
  • Unclaimed shares never expire.
  • CURB can also freeze payouts: what CURB can and cannot do.
