LONGER DESCRIPTION - site, Farcaster, anywhere with room
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CURB is a launchpad on Base where tokens are paired with tokenized stocks
  instead of with ETH.

  The name is the Curb Exchange: the traders who could not get inside the New
  York Stock Exchange dealt on the kerb outside, and eventually became the
  American Stock Exchange. "Kerb trading" is still the term for dealing outside
  official market hours - which is what onchain equities are, all the time.

  Every launch trades against a real ticker, so a token's chart is priced in
  something with a price of its own rather than in the market's mood. When a
  launch completes, its liquidity moves into a Uniswap v4 pool in one
  transaction and the position is locked; the part of the supply the pool
  cannot absorb is burned rather than held by anyone.

  Being built in public. Nothing is live.

RE-RENDERING THE PNGs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Serve this folder, put the SVG in a bare page at its natural size, and
  screenshot it headless:

    python -m http.server 8778 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory brand
    chrome --headless --disable-gpu --hide-scrollbars \
      --force-device-scale-factor=1 --window-size=1500,500 \
      --screenshot=out.png "http://127.0.0.1:8778/page.html"

  ONE TRAP, ALREADY HIT: a linearGradient on a perfectly horizontal path needs
  gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse". The default is objectBoundingBox, that path's
  bounding box has zero height, and the gradient is then undefined - Chrome
  draws nothing at all, so the painted line silently vanished from the export
  while still looking correct in some viewers.
